### Allow Rannah رنّة to take over conversations (recommended)

<Note>
  This step is **optional but strongly recommended** — and **required if you have other Meta apps connected to the same page/Instagram account** (for example Metricool, ManyChat, Chatfuel, etc.).

  Without it, when an admin opens a conversation in the Instagram mobile app, Pages Manager, or Meta Business Suite Inbox, Meta hands ownership of that thread to the Page Inbox for ~24 hours and the bot **cannot reply** until you turn this on. You may also see a "thread ownership" error email from Rannah رنّة when this happens.
</Note>

- Go to [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com) and **select the business portfolio** that owns your connected Facebook/Instagram pages.
- Click the **gear / Settings icon at the bottom left** to open Business settings.
- In the left sidebar open **Integrations → Conversation routing**.

![Conversation routing — partner apps list](/assets/meta/take-over-chat-1.png)

- In the list of profiles, **select each connected page / Instagram account one by one** (you'll need to repeat this for every page you connected to Rannah رنّة).
- Open the **Partner apps** tab and find **U Connector** in the list, then click **Manage**.

![Manage U Connector — toggles disabled](/assets/meta/take-over-chat-2.png)

<Warning>
  By default both toggles are **OFF** as shown above. With both toggles off the bot cannot reliably reply to conversations whenever another app or the Meta Inbox touches the thread.
</Warning>

- **Turn ON both toggles**:
  - **Access all conversations** — lets U Connector see every DM, even ones currently routed to another app or the Inbox.
  - **Take control of conversations** — lets U Connector automatically take ownership of a thread back from another app/Inbox so the bot can reply.

![Manage U Connector — both toggles enabled](/assets/meta/take-over-chat-3.png)

- Press **Save**.
- **Repeat for every Instagram / Facebook page** in the Profiles list on the left that you've connected to Rannah رنّة.

<Tip>
  Once both toggles are enabled, Rannah رنّة will automatically reclaim thread ownership behind the scenes whenever it gets stolen by the Meta Inbox or another connected app — you should not need to touch this again unless you connect a new page.
</Tip>

### Notes, Meta rules & restrictions:

- **We automatically transcribe any voice messages to text for the AI agent**, you can disable this option in the agent > channels tab. The original audio is still stored and shown in the [Conversations tab](/features/conversations-tab) alongside the transcription.
- Human handoff supports both facebook & instagram channels & 2 way text/images support from the dashboard.
- **Instagram web for some reason has a lot of issues with buttons not working properly or not showing up at all, we recommend using the mobile app for the best experience espeically during testing.**
- Meta has a lot of restrictions on new accounts especially if you're trying to connect instagram pages, **make sure to enable 2FA on both your facebook & instagram accounts.**
- There will always be a **24 hour window for sending messages** to any fb/ig pages after that you can only send messages to the page if the user has sent a message to the page first, this is a restriction by meta.
- **Meta requires you to always let the end user know that they're talking to a bot/AI agent & if a human handoff happened it is also a requirement to mention that they are no longer are speaking to a bot**, this is a requirement by meta and we automatically handle it for you.
- **If requested by the end user we will automatically delete any data we have on them from the conversation.**

<Info>
  **Message Length Limit:** Facebook Messenger supports 2,000 characters per message, while Instagram DMs are limited to 1,000. We automatically split outgoing text at natural boundaries so longer replies still deliver in full on both channels.
</Info>
