## Overview

Voice is billed per minute. **Total ≈ platform fee + provider rate(s)**.

- **1 credit = $0.001** · **1,000 credits = $1**
- Platform fee: **20 credits / min ($0.020)**
- Provider rates below are platform USD/min (same catalog as the public pricing page)

Voice / phone unlocks on **Business+** (or the Voice add-on). See [Voice pricing](../voice/pricing).

## Choosing providers

- **Budget STT:** Speechmatics or Google Cloud Speech
- **Budget TTS:** Google Cloud TTS
- **Realtime all-in-one:** Gemini Live
- **Premium voices:** ElevenLabs / PlayHT (higher per-minute cost)
