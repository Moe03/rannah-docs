## How to Reach Us

We're here to help! Whether you have questions, need technical support, or want to share feedback, use the channel that fits your situation.

---

### 💬 In-App Support (Recommended)

<Card>
  Open the Rannah رنّة dashboard and select **Support** in the left sidebar under the **System** group (`/app/[region]/support`).
</Card>

The in-app Support page embeds Rannah رنّة's support agent so you can describe your issue in chat without leaving the dashboard. Your workspace name, email, subscription status, and region are passed to the agent automatically so you do not need to repeat account details.

<Note>
  A workspace email is required before the Support page loads. If you see a loading state, confirm your workspace profile includes a valid email address.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Paid subscribers and custom-plan workspaces receive **priority support** routing in the in-app agent context.
</Tip>
