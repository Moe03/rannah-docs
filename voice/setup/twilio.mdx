Integrate **Twilio** with the Voice Suite to enable seamless phone number management and real-time calling. Follow the steps below to configure Twilio in your Rannah رنّة dashboard.

<Note>
  On **v2-2026**, Voice / phone starts on **Business** (2 Twilio slots included) or via the Voice add-on. Extra number slots are $3/mo. See [Voice pricing](/voice/pricing).
</Note>

---

## 🛠 Step 1: Access the Voice Setup Page

1. Navigate to the **Voice Setup** page in the Rannah رنّة dashboard.
2. Select the **Phone Numbers** tab.

![Voice Setup Page](/voice/assets/select_number.png)

---

## 📞 Step 2: Purchase or View Twilio Numbers

### 2.1 **View Available Numbers**

- Click on the **Phone Numbers** tab.
- A list of **Available Numbers** will appear.
- To refresh the list, click **"Refresh List"**.

### 2.2 **Purchase a Number**

- Click **"Buy"** next to a Twilio number to purchase it.
- Once purchased, the number will appear under **Purchased Numbers**.

![Available and Purchased Twilio Numbers](/voice/assets/twilio-ss.png)

---

## 🔑 Step 3: Assign a Number to Your Agent

1. Under **Purchased Numbers**, locate the number you wish to assign.
2. Click the **"Assign"** button.
3. Assign the number to your virtual agent (e.g., **test**).

> **Tip**: You can **Release** or **Unassign** numbers if needed.

![Assigning a Twilio Number](/voice/assets/purchase_number.png)

---

## 📥 Step 4: Import Your Own Twilio Number

If you already own phone numbers in your Twilio account, you can import them instead of purchasing through Rannah رنّة.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the agent Voice settings">
    In the dashboard, go to **Agents** → select your agent → **Voice** → **Phone Numbers** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the import">
    Click **Import Twilio Number** in the top-right of the Phone Numbers section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Twilio credentials">
    Fill in:
    - **Phone Number** — E.164 format (e.g. `+14155551234`)
    - **Account SID** — from your [Twilio Console](https://console.twilio.com) dashboard
    - **Auth Token** — from the same dashboard (stored encrypted by Rannah رنّة)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select capabilities">
    Enable **Voice**, **SMS**, or both. At least one capability is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">
    Click **Import Number**. Rannah رنّة configures voice/SMS webhooks and assigns the number to the **current agent**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The import is scoped to the agent whose Voice settings you opened. If you manage numbers for multiple agents, repeat the import from each agent's Phone Numbers tab.
</Note>

Imported numbers appear in your workspace phone list with an **imported** indicator. Outbound test calls from the Voice tab are only available for imported numbers.

For API-based imports, see [Import a Twilio number](/api-reference/v3/voices/import-twilio-number).

## 🚦 Troubleshooting

### Issue: Twilio Numbers Not Showing Up

- Ensure your Twilio credentials are correct.
- Refresh the list under **Available Numbers**.

### Issue: Calls Not Connecting

- Verify that your assigned Twilio number is active in the Twilio Console.
- Check network settings and webhook configurations.

### Issue: Imported Number Not Working

Imported numbers have extra Twilio-side requirements (routing status, webhooks, emergency address). See the dedicated guide: [Troubleshooting Imported Twilio Numbers](/support/imported-twilio).

Common causes:

- **Inactive regional routing** in the Twilio Console (must show **Active**)
- **Missing or incorrect webhooks** on the phone number
- **Voice capability not enabled** on the imported number
