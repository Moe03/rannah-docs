Learn how to enable **voice-to-voice interactions** in Rannah رنّة using Web Calling. With this feature, users can make calls directly from your website using the Rannah رنّة Voice widget.

---

## 🎙 Web Calling

Web Calling allows seamless voice communication from your widget. Follow these steps to enable the feature:

1. Navigate to the **Voice Setup** page in your Rannah رنّة dashboard.
2. Go to the **Advanced & Settings** tab.
3. Enable **"Web Calling"** by toggling the switch.

![Web Calling Settings](/voice/assets/webcalling.png)

> Once enabled, the widget will automatically display a **Call Button**. When pressed:
>
> - A circular **audio visualizer** will appear.
> - Users can press the **"Start Call"** button to initiate the call.

---

## 🚀 Deploy the Widget to Your Website

To integrate the widget into your website:

1. In the Rannah رنّة **Agent Dashboard**, click on **Demo** at the top right.

![Deploy Widget Highlight](/voice/assets/deploy.png)

2. Enter a website URL to see how your agent appears on it.

![Deploy Widget Highlight](/voice/assets/deplyed.png)

### Script Showcase

Here’s an example of the script:

1. In the Rannah رنّة **Agent Dashboard**, click on **Published Arrow** at the top right.

2. Press **Website Script** and copy the generated script snippet.

3. Paste the script into your website’s HTML.

   ![Script Popup Showcase](/voice/assets/deploying2.png)
